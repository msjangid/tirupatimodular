Product specifications and details :  
Delivering Comprehensive Interior Works for Turnkey Project
This turnkey project covers complete interior finishing and installation works, ensuring the site
is delivered in a ready-to-use state with premium quality and aesthetic appeal. The scope
includes false ceiling systems, ACP cladding, color painting, electrical fittings, and accessory
supply.

The false ceiling work features innovative designs using materials such as gypsum, PVC, or metal panels, enhancing lighting integration, acoustics, and overall ambience. ACP cladding covers both internal and external surfaces, offering a durable, weather-resistant, and elegant finish. Painting work includes surface preparation, primer application, and coats of high-quality paints to ensure a smooth and lasting appearance.

Electrical work involves high-standard wiring, fitting, and installation of switches, lights, circuit protection systems, and other accessories as per approved layouts and safety codes. All materials and accessories supplied comply with industry standards and are installed under expert supervision to ensure performance, safety, and longevity.

The overall execution follows strict quality control measures and precise coordination to deliver a complete, reliable, and aesthetically refined space ready for client use.

Luxury Modular Furniture:
The project also includes the design, fabrication, and installation of luxury modular furniture that 
blends functionality with refined aesthetics. Each furniture unit is crafted using premium-grade
materials and precision manufacturing techniques to ensure durability, style, and comfort.
Exclusive hardware fittings, such as soft-close hinges, telescopic channels, and imported handles,
are used to enhance both the visual appeal and operational smoothness of every unit. The
modular setup allows flexibility, efficient space utilization, and a modern look that complements
the overall interior design theme.

Antient, Royal, Teakwood Furniture:

This exclusive range brings timeless elegance and regal charm to every space, creating a warm and sophisticated ambience perfectly suited for luxury homes and elite hospitality.

The goal is to execute all tasks within specified timelines while maintaining strict adherence to safety standards, quality controls, and client requirements.
